- 🚀 I lead product at [Byro](https://usebyro.com), a community events platform for organisers and their regulars.

- 🏆 Arbitrum bounty winner at ETHGlobal Buenos Aires. Rootcamp (Rootstock developer program) graduate.
- 🌍 Currently exploring real-world asset tokenisation and stablecoin settlement.
- 👯 Open-source contributor to GoodDollar and the Stellar ecosystem.
- 🤝 Open to collaboration on Web3 projects, and to junior frontend and smart contract roles.
